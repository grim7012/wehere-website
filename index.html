Our Story & Leadership

An antidote to the loneliness of social media.

Modern social networks were built to harvest attention, optimize for outrage, and rank humans by appearance. The result is an epidemic of silent isolation: millions of people surrounded by followers, yet having nobody to talk to when life gets heavy.

We started WeHere in Lucknow with a simple, radical mission: to create a digital sanctuary where kindness is the currency, listening is celebrated, and vulnerability is met with compassion.

Safety & Values

A safer, kinder place to talk.

We put human dignity and boundary protection at the center of everything we build.

Hybrid Anonymity

Use a serene botanical handle. Your real name, phone number, and identity are never exposed unless you both mutually agree to a name reveal handshake.

Zero Tolerance for Creeps

WeHere is an emotional sanctuary, not a hookup app. Harassment or inappropriate behavior results in immediate permanent ban with 24h moderation turnaround.

1-Tap Block & Silent Unmatch

Leave any conversation quietly at any instant. Unmatching is silent and immediate — the chat disappears for both users without drama.

No GPS Hardware Tracking

WeHere never requests hardware GPS device permissions. Users choose an approximate state and city from a dropdown purely for cultural matching.

EXIF Metadata Stripping

When photos are uploaded, all embedded GPS coordinates, camera models, and device timestamps are permanently stripped before storage.

Self-Serve Data Deletion

Full compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023 Sec 12 and GDPR Art 17. Delete your profile and all associated data in app settings at any time.
