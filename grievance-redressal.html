What Can Be Reported

Users may raise complaints concerning:

privacy;

personal data;

content;

harassment;

safety;

self-harm content;

impersonation;

intellectual property;

subscriptions;

payments;

refunds;

account issues; and

other Platform matters.

Complaint Information

Where available, provide:

name;

contact details;

account details;

description;

relevant content;

URL or identifier;

date/time; and

supporting evidence.

Processing

WeHere will process grievances according to applicable legal requirements and its internal procedures.

Applicable statutory timelines will take precedence over any general timeline in this Policy.

Investigation

WeHere may examine relevant information reasonably necessary to investigate a complaint.

Resolution

Depending on the matter, WeHere may:

remove content;

restore content;

restrict content;

suspend an account;

terminate an account;

correct information;

provide an explanation;

or

take other appropriate action.

Privacy Complaints

Privacy complaints may be sent to privacy@weherenetwork.com

IP Complaints

IP complaints may be submitted with evidence establishing the complainant's rights and identifying the relevant content.

Emergency Safety

For immediate physical danger, users should contact the appropriate emergency service. WeHere may prioritise serious safety reports.
