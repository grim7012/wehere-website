1. General Overview

WeHere provides peer connection and wellness habit tracking free of charge to all users. To support our mission and provide an enhanced, uninterrupted experience, WeHere offers optional paid subscription tiers ("WeHere Premium").

This Policy sets forth the terms governing subscription purchases, auto-renewals, cancellations, and refunds across all billing channels.

2. Premium Subscription Plans & Features

WeHere Premium unlocks the following digital features:

  • 100% Ad-Free Experience across all screens and community circles.
  • Mutual Real Name Reveals in private peer conversations.
  • Unlimited Profile Swipes in the daily connection deck.
  • Priority Matchmaking Queue and Exclusive Supporter Profile Badges.

4. Payment Methods & Processing

Mobile Applications (Google Play & Apple App Store): Subscriptions purchased within the iOS or Android mobile application are billed directly through your Apple ID or Google Play account. Store policies and payment rules apply to all mobile purchases.

Web / Direct Channels (Razorpay): Where payment is accepted via our official website, transactions are processed securely via authorized payment gateways (Razorpay) under the merchant name WeHere Networks Private Limited.

5. Auto-Renewal & Cancellation

How Cancellation Works: You can cancel your subscription at any time without penalty. When you cancel, future automatic renewals are stopped immediately.

Continued Access: Your premium features will remain active until the end of your current prepaid billing cycle.

How to Cancel:

  • Apple iOS: Open iPhone Settings > tap your Name > Subscriptions > WeHere > tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Google Android: Open Google Play Store > Profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > WeHere > tap Cancel.
  • Web: Email contact@weherenetworks.com from your registered email address with your order ID.

6. Refund Policy

Digital Services Fulfillment: In accordance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 and standard digital services practices, WeHere Premium is a digital service delivered immediately upon successful payment verification. Because digital privileges are instantly unlocked and consumed, subscription fees are generally non-refundable once activated.

Eligible Refund Circumstances: We will issue a 100% refund in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Duplicate / Multiple Billing: You were charged more than once for the same subscription period due to a payment gateway error.
  • Technical Failure to Deliver: Your payment was deducted but premium features were not unlocked on your account within 24 hours of purchase, and our support team could not manually provision the membership.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for an eligible technical failure or duplicate billing:

  1. Send an email to contact@weherenetworks.com within 7 days of the transaction.
  2. Include your registered email address, date of transaction, order ID / transaction ID, and bank/payment receipt.

Verified refunds will be initiated within 3 business days and will reflect in your original payment source within 5–7 business days according to banking standards.

8. Account Termination & Banning

If an account is banned or suspended due to severe violations of our Community Guidelines, Safe Space Pledge, or Terms of Service (e.g., harassment, stalking, hate speech, or abuse), any remaining time on an active subscription is forfeited and will not be refunded.
