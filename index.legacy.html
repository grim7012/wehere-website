Our Story & Leadership

An antidote to social media loneliness.

Most social networks were built to keep you scrolling — optimizing for likes, algorithms, and curated highlights. The result is an unspoken isolation: people surrounded by followers, yet having no one to speak to when life feels heavy.

We founded WeHere in Lucknow to change that. We wanted to build a thoughtful space where being honest about your struggles is welcomed, where listening is valued, and where vulnerability is met with quiet understanding.

Safety & Values

A safer, kinder place to talk.

Clear boundaries, thoughtful privacy, and zero compromise on your safety.

Thoughtful Anonymity

Express yourself using a gentle pseudonym. Your real name, contact number, and personal details stay private, and are only revealed if both peers mutually agree.

Strict Conduct Standards

WeHere is strictly dedicated to mutual emotional support. Romantic solicitations, inappropriate messages, and harassment result in immediate permanent bans.

1-Tap Silent Unmatch

You remain in complete control of every conversation. Unmatching or blocking is instant and silent — the chat disappears quietly without alerts or conflict.

No GPS Location Tracking

We never request device GPS permissions or track your movements. You simply select a broad region from a list so we can offer relevant cultural matching.

Automatic Photo Privacy

Whenever an avatar or image is uploaded, hidden location tags, camera models, and timestamps are automatically stripped before storage to protect your privacy.

Complete Data Deletion

Your data belongs entirely to you. You can permanently delete your account, messages, and profile directly from app settings at any time with immediate effect.
