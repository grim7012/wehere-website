Our Story & Leadership

Connecting Minds.
Healing Together.

Wehere was founded on a simple, enduring conviction: everyday emotional turbulence doesn't always need a clinical label. What a person in quiet struggle needs most is simply someone kind who truly gets it.

The Leadership

Meet Our Founders

The people building a kinder, safer digital sanctuary from Lucknow, India.

K

Kanishk Kashyap

Director, CEO & Founder

Product Architecture & Vision • WeHere Networks

"There was a time in my life when I felt completely alone."

I was going through a difficult period, facing challenges that I didn't always know how to navigate. And during those moments, I realized something that fundamentally changed how I viewed the world: sometimes, what a person needs is not clinical diagnosis or unsolicited advice. They simply need someone who is there.

Traditional social platforms amplify noise, curated vanity, and outrage. We built Wehere as the antidote: a space of quiet psychological safety where you can be real, unburden yourself, and meet peers on the exact same emotional frequency.

N

Nailah Istifa

Co-founder & COO

Operations & Community Safety • WeHere Networks

"One night, while living alone in a city far from home, I felt completely disconnected."

I’ve always been an overthinker. Doomscrolling made it worse, and with no friends or family nearby, I had no one to speak openly to. That night I realized the true struggle wasn’t the world outside — it was being trapped alone with spiraling thoughts in the dark.

At Wehere, my obsession is user safety and dignity: zero tolerance for harassment, robust hybrid anonymity, and moderation systems that make sure nobody ever feels unsafe while reaching out for support.

In my work across communities and educational institutions, I constantly see the heavy emotional toll of modern deadlines and isolation. People don't always need therapy; they need authentic connection and a listening ear before burnout becomes a crisis.

Our Operating Principles

01

Kindness by Default

Zero tolerance for creeps, unsolicited dating advances, or invalidating advice. Every conversation begins with our mutual Safe Space Pledge.

02

Hybrid Anonymity

Interact via botanical pseudonym handles. Your real first name stays completely private unless both peers mutually agree to reveal it.

03

Non-Clinical & Ethical

We never impersonate doctors or therapists. We provide peer support and mindfulness habits while maintaining immediate links to 24/7 national lifelines.

04

Privacy & Self-Serve Erasure

No hardware GPS tracking, automatic EXIF image stripping, and self-serve one-tap complete account deletion under India's DPDP Act 2023.
