Our Story & Leadership

Built by people who understand what it feels like to struggle in silence.

We founded WeHere on a simple conviction: when life gets heavy, you shouldn't have to carry it alone. Everyday emotional struggles don't always need clinical labels — sometimes what helps most is simply one empathetic person who genuinely listens.

The Leadership

Meet Our Founders

The team building a calmer, safer peer support community from Lucknow, India.

K

Kanishk Kashyap

Director, CEO & Founder

Product Architecture & Vision • WeHere Networks

"There was a time in my life when I felt completely alone."

I was going through a difficult period, facing challenges that felt heavy and isolating. During those moments, I realized something that stayed with me: when you're overwhelmed, you don't always need unsolicited advice, diagnoses, or quick fixes. Often, you simply need someone who is willing to listen without judgment.

Traditional social platforms are built around performance, algorithms, and surface-level interactions. We built WeHere to be the opposite: a calm, grounded space where you can unburden your mind, speak honestly, and connect with peers on the exact same wavelength.

Operations & Community Safety • WeHere Networks

"Living alone in a new city, the hardest part was the quiet."

I've always had a tendency to overthink. Living alone in a city far from home, there were nights when scrolling through social media only amplified the silence and disconnection. I realized that the hardest part wasn't the external pressure — it was being stuck in a spiral of thoughts with no safe outlet.

At WeHere, my priority is making sure our community is genuinely safe and dignified. That means strict conduct standards, thoughtful privacy protections, and responsive moderation so no one ever feels unsafe while reaching out.

Our Operating Principles

01

Respect & Kindness by Default

Zero tolerance for harassment, unsolicited dating approaches, or dismissive attitudes. Every conversation begins with a shared commitment to mutual respect and psychological safety.

02

Thoughtful Anonymity

Interact comfortably under gentle pseudonym handles. Your real name, contact number, and personal identity remain completely private unless both peers mutually agree to share names.

03

Non-Clinical & Ethical Peer Support

We are a peer-to-peer support community, not a clinical healthcare provider. We offer supportive listening and habit tools, alongside clear, immediate access to licensed 24/7 national helplines.

04

Privacy & Data Control

No precise GPS location tracking, automatic photo metadata scrubbing, and complete self-serve account deletion under India's DPDP Act 2023.
